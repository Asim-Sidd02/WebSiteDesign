## SignSense-provided clips

Eight additional motion tracks were prepared from video clips supplied for the SignSense demo. The eight labels are loud, quiet, happy, sad, beautiful, ugly, deaf, and blind. These clips have not been independently validated by a fluent ISL reviewer.

## Scope

These are isolated word and phrase motion examples, not a trained general-purpose sign-language translation model. ISL grammar, regional variation, non-manual markers, context, and signer variation require review by Deaf ISL users and qualified language experts before real-world use.
